**The Daily World** — a one-page broadsheet front page that sums up the world news of Friday 20 November 2026 in a single 1024×1024 image. The lead is the International Court of Justice's genocide verdict against Myanmar over the Rohingya; around it sit the first Trump–Xi–Putin summit in Shenzhen, Russia's overnight strike on Kyiv and the missile that hit Poland, Cyclone Akvan's landfall in Odisha, the deepening AI sell-off as Anthropic lists, Houthi missiles over Riyadh, and fifteen wire briefs from Brussels to Nashville. `main.py` copies the finished `entry.png` into the output directory; `compose.py` is the PIL typesetter that produced the page at build time.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
